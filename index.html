Meet the members

Description

CORTIS is a five-member boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. They debuted on August 18, 2025 with the single "What You Want", and their name comes from the phrase "Color Outside the Lines", a nod to thinking freely and doing things their own way. Martin, James, Juhoon, Seonghyeon, and Keonho are known for being closely involved in the creative side of their work, from the music to the choreography to the visuals. Their fans call themselves COER. This fan page collects the basics in one place: who the members are, what they have released, and where to find official merch and concert info.
